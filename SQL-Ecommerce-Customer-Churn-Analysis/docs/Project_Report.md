# SQL E-Commerce Customer Churn Analysis
## Project Report

---

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most important business challenges for e-commerce companies. Acquiring a new customer is generally more expensive than retaining an existing one. Therefore, identifying churn patterns and understanding customer behavior can significantly improve profitability.

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior, demographics, complaints, spending habits, and engagement to identify the major factors associated with customer churn.

---

# Business Problem

The company experiences customer attrition but lacks clear insights into:

- Which customers are most likely to churn
- Which regions have the highest churn rates
- How complaints affect customer retention
- Whether discounts improve retention
- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue
- Which factors contribute to customer loyalty

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Clean and validate customer data
- Explore customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Measure customer churn
- Analyze revenue distribution
- Identify high-value customers
- Discover business trends using SQL
- Build reusable SQL views for reporting

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

### Improve Customer Retention

Focus retention campaigns on customers with long periods of inactivity.

### Reduce Customer Complaints

Improve customer support response time and issue resolution to increase satisfaction.

### Reward High-Value Customers

Offer loyalty rewards and exclusive promotions to premium customers.

### Optimize Discount Strategy

Evaluate discount effectiveness and target promotions toward customers at higher risk of churn.

### Regional Marketing

Develop region-specific marketing strategies based on churn and revenue performance.
